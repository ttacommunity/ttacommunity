This site is run by volunteers from the Through the Ages community. This page says what we keep about you, why,
who can see it, and how to have it removed. Questions and requests go to **privacy@ttacommunity.org**.

## When you visit

The ratings, tournaments and blitz pages are public and need no sign-in. They show TTA usernames and the games
and results played in the online app's tournaments.

- Browsing sets no cookies.
- Cloudflare serves the site and counts visits with its Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and, Cloudflare
  says, collects no personal data.
- Our own servers record only what kind of request was made, never your IP address or which player you looked
  up.
- Your browser remembers some display choices, such as the leaderboard's view, in its own storage. They are
  never sent to us.

## When you sign in

You sign in with your Discord account, with a link we email you, with a Microsoft account (personal, work or
school), or with a Google account. Discord tells us your Discord user id, your
username and your display name, and we keep only those. We never see your password or your servers, nor, through
Discord, your email.

If you sign in by email, we keep your email address, to send you sign-in links, and so admins and organisers
can contact you about events you enter. You can change it on your account page. Each link works once, within an
hour. To limit how many we send, we also keep each address a link went to, and when, for two days. Before
sending one, Cloudflare's Turnstile checks that a person, not a program, filled in the form.

If you sign in with Microsoft, Microsoft tells us an id it gives you for this site alone, your name, and your
email address when Microsoft says it is verified. We keep those. The email is only for admins and organisers to
contact you about events you enter: it never decides who you are. We never see your password or anything else in
your Microsoft account.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your Google account's id, your name, and your email address when
Google says it is verified. We keep those, for the same use as Microsoft's. We never see your password or anything
else in your Google account.

You may add Discord, Microsoft, Google or an email address to your account as another way in, and remove any of
them while another remains.

You then tell us your TTA username, so we can link your sign-in to you as a player. An admin or organiser checks
the link, unless an organiser has already recorded your Discord name or email address for that username. Once
linked, an email address your sign-in gave us is recorded on your player record if it has none. You may also give your
country (for the flag beside your name) and your time zone. Until the link is made they are kept with your
sign-in, and whoever checks the link sees them.

To keep you signed in, we set one cookie on api.ttacommunity.org: a random token that only our servers can read,
and of which we keep only a scrambled copy. While you sign in, a second cookie holds the sign-in's check values
for ten minutes at most. Your browser also notes that you have signed in, so pages know to ask who you are.

A session ends, and is removed, when you sign out, or after 30 days without use. Your account itself (your Discord id and names,
your email address if you sign in by email, your Microsoft or Google id, name and email if you sign in with one of
them, and the player it is linked to) stays until you delete it or ask us
to remove it.

## When you take part

When you enter a tournament or event we run, the organisers record you as a community member: your TTA username,
your Discord name, an email if you gave one, your country and time zone, and a community ban if there is one.

- **Public pages** show your TTA username and, in a tournament, your country's flag and your time zone.
- **Only admins and organisers** see your Discord name, your email, your sign-in and any ban. If you are banned,
  you see it too, with its reason, on your account page.
- Our data exports, which may be shared with the community, never include sign-ins, Discord names, emails or
  bans.
- Admin keeps a log of each change made to your record: who made it, when, and which details it changed, never
  what they were changed to.

## Who else is involved

- **Discord**, when you sign in with it.
- **Microsoft**, when you sign in with it.
- **Google**, when you sign in with it.
- **Resend**, which sends our sign-in emails, and so sees your email address and the link.
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts the site and our API and so handles every request, and runs Turnstile on the email
  form.

We never sell your data or share it with anyone else.

## Your choices

- See everything we keep about you, and when each part is removed, under **Data we keep** on
  [your account page](/account/).
- Sign out at any time there, where you can also change your country and time zone.
- Delete your account there too. Every way you sign in goes at once, with every session and your requests to be
  linked, and your Discord name, country and time zone are cleared from your player record, with the email
  address recorded there if it is one your sign-in gave us. Your TTA username, games and ratings stay:
  they come from results published in the online app. We keep a note that an account was deleted, without your
  Discord, Microsoft or Google details or email address. Our nightly backups are encrypted and each is deleted
  after 30 days, so what you delete stays in them for up to a month.
- Write to **privacy@ttacommunity.org** to correct what we hold, have the rest removed, or, without an account,
  see what we hold about you. Tell
  us your TTA username and Discord username or email address; we check it is really you, for example by a message
  on Discord or a reply from that address, before we change anything.

When this page changes, its date changes.
