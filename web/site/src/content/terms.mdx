This site is run by volunteers from the Through the Ages community. It is not run by CGE: Through the Ages is
CGE's game. By signing in or taking part in the events we run, you accept these terms. Questions go to
**support@ttacommunity.org**.

## Your account

- Have one account. It may have several ways to sign in: Discord, a link we email you, Microsoft or Google.
- Link it only to your own TTA username. Never claim another player's.
- Report only the results of games you played, as they ended.

## Ratings and results

Ratings and tournament results come from games played in the online app. They can change when we correct the
data or compute the ratings again.

## Admins and organisers

Admins and organisers may correct a result, refuse or remove a link to a TTA username, and ban a member who
breaks these terms, or whom the community has banned, from taking part in the events we run. A banned member
sees the ban and its reason on [their account page](/account/).

## Our API

The API that the site's pages read is free to use within its rate limits. What it offers is described in
[its specification](https://github.com/ttacommunity/ttacommunity/blob/main/backend/api/openapi.yaml).
