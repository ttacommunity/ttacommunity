      In <strong>2-Player Duels ($N=2$)</strong>, information scaling is $W(2) = \phi^{2-2} = \phi^0 = 1.0$. Both Glicko-2 Standard and GlickoD assign a match weight of $w = 1.0$. For established competitors, their 1v1 formulas yield <strong>mathematically identical rating updates</strong>.<br />

This doubles or triples all rating swings, artificially deflates uncertainty (RD), and inflates leaderboards.<br />
      By contrast, <strong>GlickoD</strong> deploys <strong>Golden Ratio ($\phi \approx 1.618$) Information Scaling</strong>, setting $W(N) = \phi^{N-2}$ effective match equivalents ($W(3) \approx 1.618$ and $W(4) \approx 2.618$). Combined with the <strong>15-Game Recalibration Breakthrough</strong>, GlickoD captures multilateral evidence without runaway variance or newcomer distortion.
    </p>
