<div class="faq-card">
  <h3>Why are 2p, 3p, and 4p ratings separated?</h3>
  <div>

      <p>
    Through the Ages accommodates different competitive player counts (2-player duels, 3-player, and 4-player games).
  </p>
  <p>
    The portal provides dedicated rating trackers for each player count as well as a combined <em>All Formats</em> leaderboard:
  </p>
  <ul class="faq-list">
    <li><strong>2-Player Duels:</strong> Zero-sum bilateral competition with direct military and cultural denial.</li>
    <li><strong>3-Player Matches:</strong> Dynamic multilateral balancing where trailing players naturally constrain leaders.</li>
    <li><strong>4-Player Games:</strong> Full table dynamics with rich resource flows, complex turn ordering, and multi-way tension.</li>
    <li><strong>All Formats Unified:</strong> Aggregates all competitive play, applying dynamic per-match format routing.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    This allows players and analysts to inspect skill ratings, win rates, and ranking positions across specific disciplines.
  </p>
          </div>
